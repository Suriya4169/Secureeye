Privacy Policy

Effective Date:

1. Introduction

Welcome to SecureEye – AI-Based CCTV Surveillance System. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application and software services.

2. Data Collection and Processing

SecureEye is designed with privacy as a priority. All AI processing, including person and object detection through your camera feeds, happens locally on your device. We do not stream, record, or continuously upload your live video feeds to any external servers.

3. Email Alerts and Notifications

To provide security notifications, our system uses standard email services (such as Gmail via SMTP API). When a potential threat is detected, SecureEye may capture a temporary image snapshot. This snapshot is transmitted directly to your configured alert email address. We do not intercept or store these alert snapshots on any of our own databases.

4. No Data Selling

We respect your privacy. We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information, email addresses, or camera data with any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers under any circumstances.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take steps to delete such information immediately.
