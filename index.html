Privacy Built-in.

We believe security shouldn't compromise privacy. SecureEye is architected with strict data isolation principles to ensure your footage belongs only to you.

  • AI processing happens strictly locally in your browser.
  • No continuous video footage is stored or uploaded to the cloud.
  • Only critical alert snapshots are temporarily transmitted as evidence.
  • Your data is never sold, traded, or shared with third parties.
